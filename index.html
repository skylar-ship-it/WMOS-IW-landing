The All on Everyday Protocol

Surgery in the morning. New teeth by the afternoon.

Our clinical workflow combines photogrammetry, intraoral scanning, and same-day 3D-printed temporary teeth — delivered the same day as surgery, refined during your healing phase, then finalized with permanent restorations.

1

Consult & Plan

3D imaging. Treatment plan. Honest conversation about what's possible and what isn't.

Frequently Asked

The real answers.

No hedging, no marketing speak. The questions every patient asks and what we tell them.

Single arch full-arch reconstruction at WMOS typically ranges from $25,000 to $35,000 depending on bone grafting needs, anesthesia, and the final restoration material chosen. Both arches range from $50,000 to $65,000. We offer transparent pricing during your consultation, with financing through CareCredit and other partners. We do not believe in surprise billing.

Often, yes. As board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeons, we routinely treat patients who were told they had insufficient bone, were too old, or had health conditions that disqualified them elsewhere. With modern bone grafting, zygomatic implants, and IV sedation protocols, very few patients are truly not candidates. The only way to know is a real consultation with imaging.

Most patients return to work within 3 to 5 days. Soft food diet for the first 8 to 12 weeks while implants integrate with bone. You leave the day of surgery with functional temporary teeth — no period of being toothless. Final permanent restoration is typically delivered around month 4 to 6 post-surgery.

Dentures sit on top of your gums and require adhesive. Full-arch implants are fixed permanently to titanium implants placed in your jawbone. They do not slip, click, or come out. They feel and function like natural teeth. Bone density is preserved because the implants stimulate the jaw the way natural roots do. Most patients describe the difference as transformative — and irreversible in the best way.

Three things. First, every surgery is performed by a board-certified oral and maxillofacial surgeon — not a general dentist. Second, we are local. Your surgeon will see you for follow-ups, not a rotating roster. Third, we do not pressure-sell. We will tell you honestly whether this is the right time and the right procedure for you, even if the answer is "wait" or "do something less invasive first."
